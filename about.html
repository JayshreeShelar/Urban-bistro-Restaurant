Our History

A Kitchen Born in the City

Urban Bistro started with a simple idea: serve the food we love to eat in a room we would want to stay in. A small team, a wood-fired grill and a short menu turned into a place where regulars have their own tables.

Today we still cook the same way, with real fire, fresh ingredients and no shortcuts, just with a few more seats at the table.

Mission & Philosophy

Honest Ingredients. Bold Flavor.

Our mission is to make great food feel effortless. We buy from nearby growers, cook with fire and season with confidence, then let the ingredients do the talking.

  • Seasonal produce from local suppliers
  • Sauces, breads and desserts made in-house
  • Warm hospitality at every table
