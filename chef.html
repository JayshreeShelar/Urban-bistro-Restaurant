Executive Chef

Chef Alex Carter

Alex has spent over a decade in kitchens across the city, learning that the best food is simple, confident and cooked with respect. At Urban Bistro, that means live fire, seasonal produce and zero shortcuts.

Before opening the line each day, Alex walks the market, tastes everything and rewrites the specials by hand.
